Bringing Wellness Through Yoga

Sai Yoga Shop provides yoga products and accessories for individual practitioners, yoga teachers, studios and retailers. Our collection includes yoga mats, blocks, belts, bolsters, ropes, meditation accessories and combo kits.

Whether you are building your personal practice or sourcing products for a yoga studio, our goal is to help you enquire about suitable products and available options.

We welcome retail, wholesale and bulk enquiries. Product availability, final prices, shipping charges and delivery timelines are confirmed by our team before an order is finalised.
